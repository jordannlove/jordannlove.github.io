Local Beekeeper Honey Jar Label

Graphic Designer

Context

A retired teacher turned beekeeper wanted a label designed for her honey jars to give them a professional look. She also wanted to highlight Coyne Creek in the design, a Kansas creek where her honeybees roam.

Action & Result

I iteratively designed a honey label for the beekeeper, showing a few varied options at each stage and building upon the ones she liked best. The beekeeper used my design for her honey jars and mailed me one as a holiday gift.
